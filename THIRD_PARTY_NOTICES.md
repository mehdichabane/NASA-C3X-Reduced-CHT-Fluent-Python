## NASA technical report and transcribed data

The NASA C3X geometry and experimental measurements are transcribed from:

> L. D. Hylton, M. S. Mihelc, E. R. Turner, D. A. Nealy and R. E. York,
> *Analytical and Experimental Evaluation of the Heat Transfer Distribution over
> the Surfaces of Turbine Vanes*, NASA-CR-168015, 1983.

NASA-CR-168015 is identified by the NASA Technical Reports Server as a work of
the United States Government for which public use is permitted. The repository
retains source attribution and does not claim authorship of the underlying NASA
measurements or geometry.

The report is also the primary source for the reduced internal-convection
correlation used here and for the ten per-hole `C_r` correction factors. Figure 7
reports the individual C3X passage geometry and `C_r` values; the
heat-transfer-measurement section documents the smooth-pipe Nusselt correlation
and the thermal-entry role of `C_r`.
